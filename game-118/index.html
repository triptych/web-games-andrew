The Kids Table A Thanksgiving merge game

How to Play

Grandma says the big table is full this year. Merge your way to a seat at it.

  • Tap a generator (the crate, the coop, the barrel, the craft box) to get a new item. Each has a few charges and earns them back slowly.
  • Drag two matching items together to merge them into the next tier. Two seeds become a sprout, two sprouts a mini pumpkin, and on up to a blue-ribbon pie.
  • Tap Serve on a guest when you have what they asked for, or drag the dish onto their card. Guests pay coins and stars.
  • Spend stars on a bigger table: more room, and new generators. Finish all five courses of the Dinner Plan to win.

Table manners

  • Keep two or three cells free. A full table locks up. Drag spares to the compost bin.
  • Read the orders before you merge. Merge what a guest or a goal wants; don't push a tier nobody asked for.
  • Stage your merges. Gather three or four of a tier first, then merge them in a row for a combo.
  • Mind the orphans. A top item with no partner just takes up space. Serve it or compost it.
  • Move your generators where their output is easy to reach. They can be dragged like anything else.
