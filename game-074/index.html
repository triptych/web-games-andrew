The Halo

Break a reliquary bearer's cage to win a Halo. It rides in front of you, swallows small fire, burns what it touches and fires with you. Cast it to send it ahead (or behind) where it holds the line on its own; cast again to call it home. It attaches to whichever side touches you.

Radiance

Hold the beam button to gather light — your feathers stop while you do. Release for a beam that pierces everything in its path. A full charge breaks gates of bone.

Relics

  • Seraph Flame — a fan of holy fire
  • Lance of Dawn — piercing lasers; level 2 ricochets
  • Choir of Wings — homing motes
  • Wing — faster flight (four levels)
  • Echo — a ghost of you that copies your shots (two)
  • Seeker Feathers — homing feathers
  • Aegis — absorbs three hits
  • Soul Shard — score

A second crystal of the same colour powers the Halo up; another colour changes its song. Destroy a whole squadron and it leaves a relic behind.

Learn the Pit

Every circle plays the same way every time. Watch for tells — bubbling lava, a flickering line, a red warning at the edge — and you will learn where to be.
