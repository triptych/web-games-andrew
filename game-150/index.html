Folio & Fable

A cozy bookshop at the end of a rainy lane

How to Play

Tam runs Folio & Fable, a bookshop left to her by a kind old bookseller. Old Vellum, a talking book, tells the story one chapter at a time.

  • Morning: arrange the shop. Tap the floor or the wall to place furniture, tap a shelf to fill it. Four books of one kind make a section; lamps, plants and the cat beside a shelf make it glow.
  • Open the shop. Browsers pick from the shelves and pay at the counter. Some bring a wish: tap 🔔 to serve them the right book, or take it as a commission for later.
  • Quests: give adventurers a book to carry (its virtues become their courage) and send them to find books and materials.
  • Bindery: craft books like a smith forges swords: a cover (rarity), an ink (its virtue), up to two accents. Rebind a favourite to make it stronger.
  • Evening: read one book. Skill manuals teach Tam things that last.
  • Town: every book you sell pours its virtues into Larkspur. Enough of one, and the town is inspired.

The End… and the Beginning

The Book of Larkspur is bound. Tam holds it up in the square, and the whole town reads along. Somewhere among the paper lanterns, a kind old bookseller smiles and turns the last page.
