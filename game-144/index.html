How to Play

Your guns fire on their own. Weave through the bullets: only the glowing dot at your cockpit can be hit. Brushing past bullets (grazing) fills your Burst gauge.

Arrows / WASDMove
Shift / Z (hold)Focus: slow down, tighten fire, show your hitbox
XBomb: wipe every bullet into stars
CWarp: blink forward through bullets
SpaceBurst: your pilot's super, when the gauge is full
P / EscPause
Enter / Space / ZNext line in a scene

Gamepad: stick to move, RB/X focus, A bomb, B warp, Y burst, Start pause. Touch: drag on the left half, buttons on the right.
