How to Play

You are Pip, a baby star-dragon in a fishbowl helmet. Blow bubbles to trap the Void Baron's crew, then touch the bubble to pop it. They come down as loot. Clear every enemy to scroll on to the next sector.

  • Chains: bubbles touching each other pop together, and each enemy in a chain is worth double the last.
  • Bounce: land on a bubble while holding Jump to spring off it.
  • Hurry: a trapped enemy breaks out in about 9 s, angry and fast. Take too long and the Void Wraith comes through the walls.
  • Specials: ⚡ zap bubbles fire a bolt the way you face; ✹ nova bubbles drop a fire that runs along the floor.
  • N·E·B·U·L·A: pop the six letter bubbles for an extra life and a free sector.
  • Fall out of a gap in the floor and you drop back in from the top.
