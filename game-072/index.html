RUNEBORE
The Delve of Old Thimble

A New Delver

Maren Ashdown's grandchild has come to Emberwick. What do the townsfolk call you?

Starting anew replaces your saved delve.

How to Delve

Keyboard & pad

← → / A DDrive · bore sideways into a wall
↑ / W / SpaceVanes: fly up (drinks aether)
↓ / SDrill down
E / EnterEnter a building or waystone
1 – 5Blast · Great Blast · Recall · Mend · Flask
JJournal & map
P / EscPause

Gamepad: stick or d-pad moves (up flies, down drills), A enters, X blasts, Y mends, LB recalls, RB pours a flask, Start pauses.

Touch

Drag anywhere on the left half: sideways to drive, up to fly, down to drill. Tap the rune buttons on the right to use items, and the glowing button to enter a shop.

The rules of the deep

  • Thimble can't drill up. Fly up the shafts you dig.
  • Watch the aether gauge. Run dry underground and the Guild crane hauls you up — for 15% of your crowns, and your cargo.
  • Hard rock needs a better auger. Upgrade at Fennick's Forge.
  • Bogwind explodes, boulders fall, magma burns, and the Emberdeep cooks an unwarded hull.
  • Seven relics lie in brick vaults deep below. Each one wakes a power in Thimble's heartcore.

Credits

RUNEBORE is a high-fantasy delving game in the spirit of the old Motherload, made for the web-games-andrew collection.

Every tile, model, portrait, sound and note is generated in code at load: no image or audio files. Rendered with three.js r165; sound is Web Audio.

Thimble's rivets by Pell Fennick. Story by Maren Ashdown, mostly.
