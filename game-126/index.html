Day 1

EMBERSPIRE

An endless tower. A tavern of friends. One ember at the top.

How to Play

Climb the Emberspire, an endless tower. Every floor is a turn-based battle; every 5th floor holds a mini boss and every 10th a true boss. Monsters grow stronger the higher you go.

Battle: when a hero's turn comes, choose Attack, a Skill, an Item, Defend or Flee, then a target. Faster heroes act more often. Hit weaknesses for 1.5x damage. Turn on Auto to let your party fight by itself, and Auto-climb to keep going floor after floor.

Hearthmere: recruit heroes at the Tavern; upgrade and reforge gear at the Forge; buy supplies at the Market; take quests at the Guild; learn skills and evolve classes at the Temple. One climb a day: come home in the evening, spend it with a companion, then rest.

Difficulty is chosen when you start a new game and can be changed in Settings: Story (gentle monsters, lots of healing, nothing lost when you fall), Standard, or Hard (tougher monsters, less rest, more lost when you fall, richer rewards).

While you're away the town pays a tithe for your deeds and benched heroes run errands. Claim a daily bonus each day you visit.
