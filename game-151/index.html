How to Play

Each mothership is guarded by escort waves. Every wave is a match-3 round: swap two neighbouring tiles to line up three or more of a kind.

Every match charges the Lance and fires missiles at the escorts. Shoot the wave down before the round clock runs out; the time you save is routed into one stat of your choice.

⚡ Powerbeats the mothership's armor
» Speedbeats its evasion
🔥 ❄ ϟ Elementsbeat its barrier, minus its resistances. Match what it is weak to.
🛡 Shieldblocks escort fire. Hull hits leak charge.

Match 4 for a line blast, 5 for a nova, an L or T for a cross. Chains and fast matches (a rush) multiply the charge.

After the last wave the mothership is exposed: charge a little more, then FIRE. You get one shot. It must reach all three targets to destroy the ship; the further past them, the better the grade (C, B, A, S) and the scrap you earn for upgrades.
