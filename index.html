How to wander

Mouse & keyboard

  • WASD or arrow keys to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump
  • Drag with the mouse to look around
  • Click the ground to walk there
  • Hover a statue to read about its game, click it to play
  • [ ] turn the hologram at the hub
  • M map · L library · F starred · T time · N sound
  • At the end of the fishing pier: C to cast, click or Space to strike, hold to reel, Esc to put the rod away

Touch

  • Hold the lower-left of the screen and slide to walk; push further to run
  • Drag anywhere else to look around
  • Tap the ground to walk there
  • Tap a statue to read about its game, then Play
  • Tap the brass arrows at the hub to turn the hologram
  • Tap ☆ on a game to star it; ⭐ lists your starred games

Each path from the hub leads to one kind of game and ends at a pavilion. Newest games stand nearest the hub. The vines wake up as you come near, and the garden keeps its own time: wait for dusk to see the lamps, fireflies and aurora, and stay up until midnight for fireworks. The door of the Observatory opens on a tower rather larger on the inside, five floors of strange rooms joined by a lift of light, with a great telescope under the dome at the top. A stone bridge crosses to the clock tower on its islet, and the door at its foot leads down into the Works, the machine hall that keeps the island running. On the far side of the island from the dock, a trail drops from the pavilion to a fishing pier: click the rod on its rail (or press C) to cast, strike when the float dips, then hold to keep the fish in the green band as you reel it in.
