CurioCove
A cozy museum on Puffin Isle

Welcome, Doctor!

Fresh from the university with a shiny new degree in archaeology. What should the islanders call you?

How to Play

Dig at the site on the hill: swap two neighbouring finds to line up three or more of a kind. Each dig has goals and a number of moves.

  • 4 in a row makes a Trowel that sweeps its row or column.
  • An L or a T makes a Pickaxe that breaks everything around it.
  • A 2×2 square hatches a Scarab that flies off to dig where you need it.
  • 5 in a row makes a Sunstone: swap it with a find to clear all of that kind.
  • Swap two specials together for a combo!
  • Dirt clears when you match on it; fossils hide underneath. Boulders crack beside matches. Crusted finds are stuck until matched. Relics lift out at the bottom.

Every dig earns ★ stars, coins and a find for the museum. Spend stars on Tasks to restore the halls, hire staff and build dinosaurs.
