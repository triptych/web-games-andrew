    How to Play

    You are Kit, the Junior Station Keeper. Captain Ada is away, so you run Starbase Nine for five days!

    • 👆 Tap on things to walk over and look at them.
    • 👽 Say hello to the visitors in the Docking Bay and answer their space questions.
    • 🔧 Fix what breaks with a little puzzle.
    • 🔍 Find clues to solve the mystery of the station ghost.
    • 🤖 Stuck? Tap Orby (or 🤖) for a hint. 📋 shows today's jobs.
    • ◀ ▶ The arrows on the toolbar (or at the edges of the room) walk you to the next room. 🗺️ The map takes you to any room.
    • 🎒 Your backpack holds the clues and things you find: tap one to look at it. 📓 The Notebook keeps your clues and space facts.
