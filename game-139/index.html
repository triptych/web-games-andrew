Your move

Clean Slate

A retired assassin's guide to hospitality

How to Play

You were Nightfall, the deadliest blade in the five cities. Now you run an inn, and every rival you ever crossed is stopping by, one a night.

1 · Clear their past

A rival deals a pyramid of cards. Only the open cards can be played: the front row, lit up. A card opens when both cards in front of it are gone. Clear every card to win the night.

2 · Pair to 13

9yours+4theirs= 13 ✓ 10yours+4theirs= 14 ✓

Tap a card in your hand, then an open card of theirs. Your cards meet them halfway: 12, 13 or 14 all count, and an exact 13 is a Clean pair (+1 Composure). Two of their open cards can pair too, but only to exactly 13. Kings go alone. A = 1, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13.

3 · A round

Each round you draw five cards. The banner at the top says what the rival will do when you press End round: strike you, slip a Grudge into your deck, lock cards… ⛨ Composure (food and drink cards give it) soaks blows; ♥ Resolve is your health for the whole run. Stuck? Pour one out swaps one card a round.

4 · Your past

The red ⚔ Blade cards are your old life: quick, strong, and costly. Each one raises Notoriety (every 4 makes all blows 1 harder), and a rival who sees too much knife work won't stay. Each letter says how many Blades that rival will forgive.

5 · Between nights

Win, then offer a room (their card joins your deck) or see them out for tips. By day, spend tips on staff and rooms, and do one chore: rest, polish a card, or let one go. Three acts, a boss at the end of each.
