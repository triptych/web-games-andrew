Clean Slate

A retired assassin's guide to hospitality

How to Play

You used to be Nightfall, the deadliest blade in the five cities. Now you run an inn. Every rival you ever crossed is coming by, one a night.

The night. The rival deals their past as a pyramid. A card is open once both cards below it are gone. Pair to 13 to settle cards: two open rival cards that make exactly 13, or one of your cards with an open rival card. Your cards meet them halfway: 12, 13 or 14 all count, and an exact 13 is a Clean pair (+1 Composure). Kings settle alone. Clear the pyramid to win the night.

Each round you draw a hand; the rival shows what they'll do when you end it (a blow, a Grudge in your deck, locked cards…). Composure soaks a blow. Once a round you may pour one out: discard a card and draw another (a Grudge is gone for good).

Blades are your old life: fast and strong. Every one you use raises Notoriety (every 4 makes all blows harder), and a rival who sees too many won't trust you enough to stay.

After a win, offer them a room (their card joins your deck) or see them out. By day, recruit staff, build rooms, and do one chore: rest, polish a card, or let one go.
