    How to Play

    Swap two neighbouring tokens to make a line of three or more. Every token on the board swears an oath, and a line only clears when every token in it is content. Most stones swear Kinship and match their own colour, but heroes, and the stones they bind, keep stranger vows:

    Each cleared stone strikes the enemy for the power of your party heroes of its colour. Ember beats Grove, Grove beats Tide, Tide beats Ember; Sun and Shade each beat the other. Matching a colour charges your heroes of that colour; tap a charged hero to use their skill.

    A hero on the board is bound when their oath is fulfilled: they join your roster for good (a hero you already have rises a rank). Summon at camp to call heroes to your next board. When a hero arrives, they change the stones around them.
