Doom & Doilies

Bake, stitch, decorate and host — and cackle now and then, so the kingdom never suspects.

How to Play

You are the Dread Lady Noxia, Overlord of the Realm. You would very much rather be baking. You must never, ever let anyone find out.

  • Craft — click or tap the castle (or press Space) to make 💗 charm. Craft quickly to build flow for a bigger bonus.
  • Minions make charm for you. Each hobby has its own room: Kitchen, Sewing Loft, Great Hall and Ballroom. Hire one to open a room.
  • Upgrades make everything better. Crafting in a room raises its mastery.
  • ☠ Dread is your reputation, and it fades — faster the cosier the castle gets. Cackle to keep it up. Feared and Dreaded overlords make more charm and collect 🪙 gold in tribute. Fall to Suspected and heroes come to look.
  • Visitors drop by: greet kittens, critics and bards for bonuses. Scare off heroes (tap them, or Cackle) before they see the doilies.
  • Gold buys décor (it appears in the castle) and henchmen, who run schemes: evil ones for dread and gold, secret commissions for charm and family heirlooms.
  • Relocate when the heroes get too close: start again in a new lair with ★ legend (+2% charm each) to spend in the Grimoire.
  • Your minions keep working while you're away, at half speed (upgrades help).
