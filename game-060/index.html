HOW TO PLAY

The BRICK ARMADA is marching on Earth!

Bounce the PLASMA BALL into the formation and blast it with your LASERS.

Hit things in a row without touching the paddle to build a CHAIN: every five hits is another ×1, up to ×5.

Catch falling CAPSULES. Don't let the ball fall, and don't get shot!

Every fourth stage, a GIANT BRICK BOSS. Dig to its glowing core.
