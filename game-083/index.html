WALK

WYRMZONE

A wingless dragon's road home

How to Play

The Kingdom of Varn has warred on dragons for a hundred years. Its wizards bound your wings with an iron ward and left you in the lowlands. Your home is the smoking mountain on the horizon. Walk there through their kingdom, break the engine that holds the ward, and fly.

Act I · On foot

↑ ↓ / W SWalk forward, back up
← → / A DTurn
Space / Z / clickBreathe fire (two fireballs at once)

Watch the radar: red blips are foes. Hide behind standing stones; dodge bolts by walking out of their line. Clear each march's quota of foes. A scale grows back each time.

Act II · On the wing

Arrows / WASD / mouseMove the targeting cross; your body follows it
Space / Z / clickFire (hold for a stream)

Shoot down firebolts before they reach you. Burn the crystals on the mage towers. In the gorge, climb or dive past the chains, and shoot the ward stone at its end.

Touch & gamepad

Drag on the left half: walk and turn on foot, aim in the air. Hold FIRE. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A to fire, Start to pause.

Foes

Footmen throw spears · ballistae fire bolts from range · lancers charge zig-zagging · wizards teleport and cast homing orbs (shoot them down) · trolls take three hits · war wagons fire pairs · a will-o-wisp is a 5000-point prize · griffon knights, mage towers and wall ballistae guard the sky.
