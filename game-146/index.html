Jingle Pop

Juniper the elf vs. a goblin's blizzard. Win back every ornament before Christmas Eve!

Level Map

How to Play

Grimwick blew every ornament out of the workshop. Match three or more of a colour to pop them free; anything left hanging from nothing falls too. Clear every ornament to win the level.

Mouse / fingerPoint to aim, click or let go to shoot
← → / A DAim (Shift for fine aim)
Space / ↑ / WShoot
↓ / S or tap the traySwap ornaments (1 2 3 pick one)
Q E R or tap the sackUse a power-up
P / EscPause

Gamepad: stick aims, A shoots, X swaps, Y / LB / RB use power-ups, Start pauses.

Every shot brings the snowy ceiling closer, and a shot that pops nothing counts double (the ❄ dots). If an ornament crosses the red line, the level is lost.

Specials

Power-ups

Presents and a full Christmas-spirit meter fill your sack. New ones unlock chapter by chapter.
