How to Play

The descent

Every step is a turn. Monsters act when you act. Find the stairs ▼ on each floor and go down — 100 floors through 10 worlds. Every 10th floor holds a Warden.

Your lantern is your light and your clock. It burns oil every turn; refill it with Oil Flasks. In the dark, the Hush gathers and hurts you.

Red tiles are telegraphed attacks: they land after your next move. Step off them!

Talk to Wayfarers (!) for quests, find Maren's journal pages (one per world), light braziers, open chests and vaults.
