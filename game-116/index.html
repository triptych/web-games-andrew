IT DEALS FROM BEYOND THE STARS!

How to Play

The visitors have one law: a planet is won or lost at the card table. Every creature deals its own table of cards. Your deck is the stock.

The beam

Tap a face-up table card that is one higher or one lower than the card on the beam (K and A wrap round). It flies onto the beam and becomes the new beam card, so you can keep chaining. Cards turn face up when nothing covers them.

The suits

  • Ray deals 2 + chain damage.
  • Rocket deals 1 + chain damage that goes straight through shields.
  • Shield gives 2 + chain block.
  • Atom gives 1 charge (2 from the 4th card of a chain).

The chain counts every table card you play this turn, so long turns hit hard. Clear the whole table for a Clean Sweep: 10 damage and a fresh deal.

A turn

Each turn starts with a free card from your stock on the beam, then you have 2 draws. Your own cards fire their effect when they land on the beam (a Death Ray zaps, a Force Field shields). Quick cards give the draw back. When you are out of moves, press End Turn: the creature does what its badge says. Block lasts until your next turn.

Gadgets

Spend Atom charge on gadgets: the Ray Pistol, a Wild Beam that lets any card fit, and more you find along the way.

The run

Three reels: Gila Flats, the Moon and the Mothership. Pick your road up each map: fights, ☢ elites, the diner, the Army Surplus store, strange broadcasts, treasure, and a boss at the top. Win fights to add cards, gadgets and souvenirs. Your HP carries from fight to fight.
