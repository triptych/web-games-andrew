How to Play

Robots pour into the maze through its four doors. Your laser fires by itself at the nearest one — you just move.

  • Data chips drop from wrecked robots. Collect them to level up and pick an upgrade.
  • The maze walls are electrified. They zap you — and fry robots dumb enough to walk into them.
  • Every minute the sector is purged: every robot blows and the maze is rebuilt.
  • Late in each sector the Grinner bounces in. It can't be destroyed. Your laser can only knock it back.
  • Survive five sectors to escape.
