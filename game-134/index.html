UFOops!

A slightly defective saucer. A very demanding mother.

How to Play

You are Zib, pilot of a Model 7-B saucer (one dent, one duct-tape patch). Beam specimens into your hold, then fly up under the Mother Ship to bank them. Meet her quota, then hover under her to dock before the clock runs out.

Arrows / WASDFly
Space / Z (hold)Tractor beam
X / C / ShiftDrop a specimen (bonks whatever it lands on)
P / EscPause
  • The beam overheats if you hold it too long, and slows you down.
  • Agents in black are beam-proof and tinfoil hats zap it back. Drop a cow on their heads first, then beam them up while they see stars.
  • Tanks and laser batteries can't be lifted, but a falling pig works wonders.
  • Every hit knocks a specimen loose and adds a defect: sputters, hiccups, honks. Duct tape fixes everything.
  • Mother grades each episode: the quota, variety, her special order, extras and how dented you came back.

Touch: drag on the left to fly, hold BEAM, tap DROP. Gamepad: stick, A to beam, B to drop, Start to pause.

Art painted with fal. Music, sound effects and the voices of Mother, Zib and General Grumbles made with ElevenLabs.
