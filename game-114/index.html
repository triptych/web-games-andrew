How to Play

Run, jump and shoot your way to the end of each sector. Shoot open the cocoon pods to free colonists: each one patches you up. Pick up weapon capsules: S Spread, L Lance (pierces), R Rocket. You keep a weapon until you lose a life.

Arrows / WASDMove and aim (8 ways)
DownCrouch (or aim down in the air)
UpAim up (stand still to point straight up)
Z / Space / KJump · hold to jump higher
Down + JumpDrop through a platform
X / JFire (hold)
C / LThrow a flare: it lights the room, then blows up and wipes out shots
P / EscPause

Touch: drag on the left to move and aim; FIRE, JUMP and FLARE on the right. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A jump, X or RT fire, B or RB flare, Start pauses.

Green beacons are checkpoints. Acid and steam hurt; pits cost a hit. Bosses lock the room until one of you falls.
