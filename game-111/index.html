NEON ARCANA

The magic has gone out of the world. Fill the lattice, rekindle the twenty-two Major Arcana, and bring it back.

How to Play

Drag a glyph from your hand onto the lattice. Fill a whole row or column and it bursts into light. When all three glyphs are placed you are dealt three more. Glyphs never rotate, unless the Wheel turns them. The reading ends when nothing in your hand fits.

Light fills the arcanum at the top. Gather enough and the card is rekindled: it returns to the sky, the world grows brighter, and the next card brings a harder omen. Light you gather is never lost: a reading that ends keeps its embers for the next.

  • Resonance — clears in a row multiply your light. A move that clears nothing fades it by one.
  • Sigils ✺ — clear a line through one for bonus light.
  • Blight — dark stones that fill a line but crack instead of clearing. Break them with a second clear.
  • Sparks ✦ — earned by rekindling, clearing three lines at once or emptying the lattice. Spend them on the powers the Arcana grant.

The World

The last card turns. Light runs back through the lattice, up into the sky, into the rivers and the lamps and the hands of everyone who had forgotten it. The twenty-two Arcana hang in the heavens, whole again, and magic is back in the world.
