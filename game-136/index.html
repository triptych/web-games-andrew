          How to Play

          Each room is for someone. Read their brief, then:

          1. Pinch: press ＋ to open the Clay Bench, pick a thing, its size, shape and colours, and pinch it out of clay. Bigger things cost more clay.
          2. Place: tap it on your shelf, then tap the floor (or a wall for pictures and clocks). Small things like lamps, plants and toys go on tables and shelves too.
          3. Finish: press ✓ to be rated. Meet every need for a star; the wishes and a stylish, cosy room earn the rest.
          4. Photograph: 📷 turns the room, sets the light (try night with lamps on), adds a filter and a miniature blur, and saves or shares a picture, or a link to the diorama itself.
