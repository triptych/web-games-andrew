Willow Halt

    Rainglass

    a night train · a misted window · one drop of rain

    How to Play

    The train has stopped. The window is misted over. Guide your raindrop across the glass to wipe it clear and see what is out there.

    • Steer the drop: drag on the window (or hold the mouse button), or use the arrows / WASD / a gamepad stick.
    • Wiping uses water. Roll over the little rain beads to drink them up. Run dry and a fresh drop beads at the top.
    • Too much water and it slides. A heavy drop runs down the glass and off the sill, wiping a clean line as it goes.
    • Tap the glass (Space, or the tap button) and nearby beads roll to your drop.
    • Spot things: each view hides three curios. Uncover them yourself before the mist melts for a gold seal on the postcard.
    • Flow: keep wiping fresh glass and the music fills in around you.

    Clear most of the pane and the rest melts away. Every window becomes a postcard in your album. There is no timer and nothing to lose.
