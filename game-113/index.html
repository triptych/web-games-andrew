How to Play

Exploring

Arrows / WASDWalk
Space / Enter / ZTalk, open, confirm
M / TabBand menu (items, charms)
P / EscPause

Walk into roaming monsters to duel them. Touch a Song Crystal to save and rest. Talk to Otto to travel.

Duels

A duel is a song. In YOUR TURN your notes fall in each princess's lane: hit them on the line to strike. In GUARD the foe's spiky notes fall: hit them to block (a perfect block parries). A missed spike hurts the princess in that lane.

D F J K  or  ← ↓ ↑ →Lanes 1–4
SpaceCrescendo when the ★ is full
HoldLong notes: keep the key down to the end
  • Lyra · Beat — the strongest taps.
  • Seraphine · Melody — heals whoever is hurt most.
  • Marigold · Lyrics — her words weave a ward that soaks blows.
  • Ingrid · Harmony — long notes that hit hard and boost the band.

Touch: drag the left half to walk, tap A. In duels, tap the lane pads under the notes, and ★ for Crescendo. Gamepad: stick, A, Y for the menu; lanes on d-pad ←/↓ and X/B.

Settings

If notes feel late, raise the offset; if early, lower it. Graphics changes apply on reload.
