History

How to Play

Three hundred years ago the Cinereal Order locked the world's magic behind seven wards, and the colour drained out of everything. You have found the last sorcerer's book. It has found you back.

The story

Read, and choose. Kindness and courage feed your hope; mercy shown to your enemies is remembered too. Who lives, who turns, and how the world remembers its long grey sleep all depend on what you did on the way.

The wards

Each ward is a lock of stone rings. Turn every ring until its key rune sits in the window at the top. Some rings drag others with them: that is the puzzle. Drag a ring round, tap its left or right side, or use the buttons. Hint shows a good next turn; Codex opens the ward for you. Each ward gives one colour back to the world.

The fire

Some nights you make camp, and can sit with two of your companions. Their stories are told over several nights, and the last of each changes how theirs ends.
