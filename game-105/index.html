Any device, any difficulty, any seed. No story, no timer.

How to Play

Customers bring broken machines. Open them, find what is wrong, fix it, put every screw back and run the power test.

Drag bare benchOrbit the camera (wheel zooms; right-drag pans; two fingers pinch)
Hold on a screwTurn it. Slide to the next one to keep going
Tap a partLift it out. It sticks to the cursor: tap again to put it down
Tap a plugPull a cable out; drop it on the socket with its colour and letter
1 – 9Choose a tool. Smart tools pick the right one for you
Q / E   Z / XRotate and zoom the camera
T / H / RPower test / hint / reverse the tool
P / EscPause

Nothing comes out while it is screwed down, plugged in or covered. Burnt-out parts have to be replaced from the drawer; some failures are invisible until you check them with the meter. A tidy bench and a clean job earn more stars.
