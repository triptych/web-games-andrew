The Hero's Merchant

Buy low. Sell high. Outfit a legend.

How to Play

You are a travelling merchant with a handcart, a ledger and a dream: to become the Hero's Merchant. Kaelen Dawnward will one day ask you for the Ultimate Kit, ten pieces of gear, and you will be rated on how well you make them.

  • Trade. Every town has shops with their own prices. Buy where a thing is made (cheap), sell where it is wanted (dear). Prices move when you trade, and events like festivals and blights shift them.
  • Pack. Your wagon is a grid: items take rectangles (press R while dragging to turn one), small goods stack, and weight slows you down. Mark favourites (protected) and junk (sold in one click). Guild Houses keep a stash.
  • Craft. Workshops turn materials into gear, levels 1-10. Better workshops are in bigger towns; better materials are further away.
  • Quests & heroes. Notice boards, townsfolk and visiting heroes all want things. Heroes pay a premium for gear.
  • Level up. Merchant level opens new regions, better wagons and recipes.

Keys: I wagon, Q quests, L ledger, M map, Esc close or menu. Touch: tap everything; drag items to rearrange them.
