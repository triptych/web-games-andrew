The Lantern Library

How to Play

You are Vellumvale's new Librarian. The Lantern Library is empty: its twelve grimoires fled when it fell, and each one ran a little wild. Find them, calm them, and bring them home. Wick, the fire sprite in the hearth, will help.

Arrows / WASDWalk
Space / Z / EnterTalk, read, open, use a grimoire's power
I / TabSatchel: grimoires, treats, gear, shelves, journal, map
P / EscPause

Grimoires lend you powers. Face fog, brambles, boulders, rivers and seals and press Action. Some places only open once you hold the right book.

Squabbles are turn-based and gentle: Shush a runaway book until its mischief runs out and it settles back into a book for your shelves. Read a foe to learn what calms it. Run out of composure and Wick simply takes you home for tea.

Far from home? Wick can take you back to the library from anywhere (Satchel → Journal), and the study hearth carries you by hearth-smoke to towns you've already visited.

Touch: drag the left half to walk, tap A. Gamepad: stick, A, B, Start.
