      How to Play

      Your guns fire on their own. Move to dodge, hold MAGNET to pull in voxels from range (you move and shoot slower while you hold it), and fire OVERDRIVE when the gauge is full.

      Every monster you destroy bursts into voxels. Red = XP (level up mid-sortie), blue = shield, green = repair, gold = credits. Walk into them, or magnet them. Voxels you leave lying around are temporary walls: monsters and their shots can't pass them (yours can), until they fade or get chewed through.

      Rockets and lasers break the ground. Blast a road through rock, or a wall into rubble cover.
