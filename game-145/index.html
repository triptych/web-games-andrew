How to Play

Nobody is here. Nothing will hurt you. Walk, look, read. Find the diaries of the missing: the brass compass always points at the next one, and an arrow under it says how many floors up or down.

Touch: drag on the left to walk, drag on the right to look, USE to use. Gamepad: sticks, A to use, X flashlight, Y journal, Select map, Start pause.

Some doors are boarded, chained, or want a card. The tools are where the missing left them. Rotten floors give way: you will fall a storey, not die.

Every city is built from a seed. Surfaces, portraits and pictures painted with fal; cars, vans, trucks and buses modelled with Meshy; the city, its buildings, light and sound are made in code.
