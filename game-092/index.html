The Expedition

Deep in the neon jungle, 24 treasures lie scattered across 96 screens. You have 20 minutes and 3 lives. Find them all before the sun sets.

Survive

  • Jump logs (they cost points) and cobras and fire (they cost a life).
  • Swing over wide pits on the vine: jump into the rope, jump again to let go.
  • Cross crocodiles only while their jaws are shut.
  • Take ladders down to the tunnels: they are a shortcut, but scorpions and walls wait there.

How to Play

Find all 24 treasures before the clock runs out. The jungle is a ring of 96 screens: run off one edge and you arrive at the next.

  • Logs cost 50 points and a stumble. Jump them.
  • Cobras, fire and scorpions cost a life.
  • Tar, quicksand and water cost a life. Swing across wide pits on the vine: jump into the rope, jump again to let go.
  • Crocodiles are stepping stones, but only with their jaws shut.
  • Ladders lead down to the tunnel. Underground, each screen you cross is three on the surface. Bricks block the way.
  • Treasure in a row builds a streak bonus, and every 6th treasure gives an extra life.

Touch: drag on the left to run (up and down climb), tap JUMP. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A to jump, Start to pause.
