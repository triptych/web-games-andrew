    Wrack & Wonder

    The first person in the world to learn magic will have to work it out alone.

    A New Island

    Every life on Hum has its own laws of magic. What you learned last time will not help you here.

    How to Play

    You are shipwrecked on an island that hums. Survive, find the hut on the bluff, and use the instruments inside to work out how magic works here. Then use it to free the girl trapped in the castle.

    Magic, briefly

    A spell is written in marks: one ringed shape (how it is thrown) and one or two bare words (what it does). Every word must be fed a reagent that carries it. Which mark means what, which end the shape goes on, and what each reagent carries are different on every island. The alembic, the bells and the orrery will tell you; so will failure. Your notebook remembers everything you have seen.
