HOLLOWDEEP

There is no bottom. Only deeper.

How to Play

Hire up to five delvers at the Guild — three fight in the front row, two shoot and cast from the back. Shop at Odo's, rest at the Inn, raise the fallen at the Temple, take work at the Tavern and the townsfolk, then walk through the Gate.

Every fifth floor a guardian blocks the stairs. Beat it and Old Ansel's warp stones will send you straight past it next time. The dungeon has no bottom anyone has found.
