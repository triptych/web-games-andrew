PERIHELION

A twin-stick odyssey across the moons of the outer dark

How to Play

You stand on a tiny asteroid. You stay in the middle of the screen; the rock turns under your feet as you walk. Find your way through the maze on its surface, silence every beacon, and the black gate rises. Step into it to travel on to the next asteroid. There are eight.

WASDWalk
MouseAim; hold the button to fire
Arrows / IJKLAim and fire
Space / Shift / right clickDash (you can't be hit mid-dash)
P / EscPause

Gamepad: left stick walks, right stick aims and fires, A or a bumper dashes, Start pauses.
Touch: drag on the left to walk, drag on the right to aim and fire, tap DASH.

Beacons launch machines from their hangars when they see you. Bolts fly round the rock on great circles, so look behind you. Red crosses repair your suit; amber crystals strengthen your gun. The arrows at the edge of the screen point the shortest way to what is left.
