HAYATERIVER STRIKE

How to Play

Sail the Hayate down three river sectors to Commodore Kurogane's dam. Shoot everything, keep the tank topped up, jump what you can't shoot, and beat the boss at the end of each sector.

← → / A DSteer
↑ / WThrottle (faster, drinks fuel)
↓ / SBrake (sit on a depot to refuel)
J / Z / Enter (hold)Cannons (or turn on auto-fire)
K / XHoming missile
Space / ShiftJUMP: fly over mines, booms, sandbars and bullets
P / EscPause
MMute

Fuel: it drains as you go. Sail over FUEL depots (brake on them), grab green energy cells, or sink a fuel barge. Shooting a depot destroys it! An empty tank doesn't kill you outright: the hull starts to fail.

Jump: the JUMP meter refills over time and with every kill. In the air you clear mines, booms, sandbars and every water-level shot and laser. Upgrades add a landing shockwave and a shell barrage.

Telegraphs: red aim lines and flashing guns show where a shot is going. Tanks paint a laser first. Bosses warn with red lanes before their beams fire.

Boosts: O Overdrive (speed + invulnerable), S Spread, P Plasma, R Rapid fire, B Barrier, M Magnet, J Jump cell, + Repair, E Energy cell.

Cores ◆ drop from kills. They are banked at every dock and when a run ends; spend them on the Skill Tree from the title screen or the dock.

Touch: drag the left half to steer (up = throttle, down = brake). Tap JUMP, MSL; hold FIRE, or let auto-fire shoot for you. Gamepad: stick, A jump, B/LB missile, X/RB/RT fire, Start pause.
