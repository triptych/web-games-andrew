Wanderwain

an idle caravan RPG

How to Play

Your grandmother's wagon, the Wanderwain, drives itself. It travels from town to town, trades, fights whatever comes down the road and takes on quests, even while the game is closed. Come back each day to see what happened.

  • Travellers join from taverns and the road. Each one fights and brings a cart: a Cook turns meat into rations, a Smith forges gear, an Alchemist brews potions, a Healer patches you up, a Trader gets better prices, a Scout finds caches, a Bard plays for coin.
  • Level the caravan for room for more travellers and better carts. Upgrade the Wanderwain's wheels, hold, larder, banner and Great Lantern, which sets how long it keeps travelling while you're away.
  • Standing orders on the Road tab: follow quests, chase trade, explore, or hold in town. Tap a town on the Map to set a course.
  • Wardens guard the six dark Waystones. Check your odds in a capital's Town tab and challenge them to open the next region.
  • Open the morning post every day for a gift (more each day in a row) and three contracts.
