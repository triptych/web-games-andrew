# Dawnshard Saga — game plan

## Pitch
A traditional fantasy JRPG in the HD-2D style: hand-drawn-looking pixel sprites standing in a real three.js diorama (tilt-shift depth of field, bloom, dynamic sun and lamps, water, lava, drifting particles). Eight heroes, a story of twenty-five beats across a seamless overmap, five generated towns with notice boards and villagers who carry three-part storylines, seven dungeons, and Octopath-style battles: shields, weaknesses, Break and Boost. Every sprite, tile, model and note is generated by code; there are no asset files.

## Story
The Dawn Crystal shattered on the eve of the thousandth sunrise and the sun stopped rising. Kael, a smith's apprentice whose chest glows with the first Spark, gathers the six shards across the world with a party that grows from one to eight — Lyra the ranger, Thorne the archer, Elara the cleric, Brom the dwarf, Mira the thief, Zephyr the scholar, Garrick the knight — and climbs the Obsidian Spire to face the Hollow King, Malachar. The ending has a real choice (share the light across the party or let one carry it). After the credits an optional super-boss waits in Cinder Maw and every board reopens.

## Modules (`js/`)
- `sim/` — the pure, seeded simulation. `game.js` (step/act/objective/serialize), `state.js`, `scene.js` (cutscene runner), `field.js` (walking, collision, interaction, encounters), `battle.js` (the rules), `party.js` (stats, equipment, XP), `quests.js`, `npcs.js`, `bot.js`; `data/` tables (classes, items, enemies, story beats, villager text); `world/` generators (overworld, towns, dungeons, navigation).
- `view/` — three.js. `pixel.js` (painter), `heroes.js` / `monsters.js` (procedural sprites), `texatlas.js`, `geo.js` / `props.js` / `mapmesh.js` (merged vertex-coloured geometry), `sky.js`, `stage.js` (post stack), `actors.js`, `fx.js` (GPU particles and mesh effects), `biomes.js`, `battle3d.js` (the battle director), `view.js`.
- `ui/` — DOM windows: `ui.js` (HUD, dialogue, title, settings), `menus.js` (pause menu, shops, boards), `battleui.js` (command windows, party panel, results).
- `audio.js` — Web Audio: look-ahead sequencer with composed melodies, SFX. `input.js`, `save.js`, `main.js`.

## Rules the layout keeps
The sim never touches the DOM, the clock or `Math.random`; the view reads `G` and drains `G.events`; one fixed step (1/120); presses queue in a FIFO; storage is guarded; the canvas is sized in px from `visualViewport`; touch is first-class; `?debug=1` exposes `window.__ds`.

## Balance
`node game-093/dev/simtest.mjs` plays the whole game with the bot and prints minutes, average level, deaths and battles at every story beat. Bosses are tuned so the bot, which does not exploit weaknesses as well as a person, wins within a couple of retries at the recommended level; a person who reads the shields and uses Break needs far less grinding.
