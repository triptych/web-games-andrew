Atlas of Ink

Chart the kingdom. Mind the reefs.

How to Play

You are a cartographer with a scrying quill. Six clients each want a map of a hidden part of the kingdom, and each will use it: the adventurer walks the road your chart shows, the captain sails it. Chart truly.

Hold the scry key to focus and release to cast. Standing still builds focus; moving bleeds it. A steady cast is wide and true. A rushed one is small and smudged, and it cannot see a town, ore vein or reef at all. Faded ink is ink you are unsure of, and a surer cast can redo it.

Arrows / WASDMove the quill
Hold Space / ZFocus, then release to scry
Enter / EHand over the chart
P / EscPause

Touch: drag on the left half to move, hold SCRY to focus and let go to cast, tap ✓ to hand over. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A to scry, Y to hand over, Start to pause.
