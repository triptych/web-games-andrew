GIANT'S VAULT

Bind the chained giant. Then go deeper.

Swear your oath

At level 6 you swear a second oath and become a hybrid: Thunderborn, Reaver or Eclipse Seer.

Gold and health globes are picked up as you walk over them. Loot that passes your loot filter (Settings) is too. When your bag fills, Ember carries the rest; send Ember to town to sell it all.

Touch: drag on the left to walk; the skill buttons aim at the nearest enemy. Gamepad: left stick, A X Y B RB for skills, LT potion.
