How to Play

You keep the quest desk of Lanternhall. Each day:

  1. Hear petitioners (Desk). Read what they say and grade the job: the creature, how many, where, and any omen each add threat; every two points is a rank. The 📖 Handbook has the tables.
  2. Send adventurers (Board). Pick up to three for each posted job. Their rank and their skill at the kind of work decide the odds. They are away for the days the job takes.
  3. Spend your afternoon (Guild). Each free action ⏳ is a visit to someone, or a mini-game in one of the rooms.
  4. End the day. Parties come home, the tavern pays in, wages go out.

Good work earns merit; every sixth day Pell reviews you for promotion. Townsfolk, the Crown, the Wild Folk and the Fellowship each trust you more or less, and on day 29 they vote.
