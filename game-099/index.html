    How to Play

    Arrows / WASDWalk (Console and games)
    Space / ZButton A (hold to keep firing)
    X / KButton B
    P / EscPause

    Console: walk into a machine. The Shelf holds your cartridges, the Stage is where you stick characters into scenes, the Press is where you make and change stickers from blocks, and the TV plays the cart.

    Stage: pick a sticker, tap the picture to stick it down. Hit ▶ and they come alive. Press: every sticker is a stack of rules like when I touch a hero → hurt it. Drag blocks into a rule, or tap one to add it.

    Touch: drag on the left of the picture to walk, A and B are buttons, and you can tap stickers. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A, X, Start.
