# Background: shop_workroom

**Location:** Wisteria's sealed workroom behind the far cellar door — locked the
week she got ill, opened for the first time by the player with the Cellar Key.
Everything is exactly where she left it. Currently reuses `cellar.png` with its
own gradient (`#241f2e` → `#08070c`).
