# Background: deep_whisperwood

**Location:** The deep Whisperwood — Phase 3's late-game area with the Thornback
Boar and Deep-Wood Stalker encounters. Should feel notably darker/wilder than
`whisperwood_edge`. Current CSS stand-in: near-black green gradient (`#16281d` →
`#030503`).

**Prompt:**

> A painterly digital illustration, storybook-style wide establishing shot (no
> characters in frame) of the deep interior of an old, strange forest, far past
> any village path. Massive ancient tree trunks crowding close together, almost
> no sky visible through the dense canopy, faint unnatural pale-glowing flowers
> or moss providing the only real light in patches, deep furrows dug into the
> earth, an unsettling stillness. Should feel notably darker, older, and stranger
> than a normal forest edge — something here doesn't quite follow natural rules.
> Color palette dominated by near-black deep greens (#0e1812 to #030503 range)
> with small eerie pale-glow accent lights (soft moon-petal white/lavender).
> Landscape aspect ratio (16:9). Moody fantasy tone bordering on eerie, not anime,
> not photorealistic. No people or creatures in the shot.
