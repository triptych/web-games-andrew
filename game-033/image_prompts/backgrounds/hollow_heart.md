# Background: hollow_heart

climax location. A flat table-sized stone with a shallow fire-bowl cut into its
top and a boundary mark weathered around the rim, in a clearing ringed with
ancient ash. Cold and full of four months of rainwater when the player first
finds it. **No painted art exists yet** — gradient-only (`#2e3b43` → `#0c110e`).
