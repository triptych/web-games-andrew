# Background: shop_interior

**Location:** The player's inherited apothecary shop — cluttered, homey, a little
run-down, main setting of the opening chapters. Current CSS stand-in: multi-stop
plum-purple gradient (`#35294a` → `#14101c`).

**Prompt:**

> A painterly digital illustration, storybook-style wide establishing shot (no
> characters in frame) of the interior of a small, cluttered countryside
> apothecary shop at dusk. Wooden shelves crowded with labeled jars of dried
> herbs, tinctures in colored glass bottles, hanging bundles of drying plants
> from low ceiling beams, a well-worn wooden counter with a brass scale, a single
> warm lamp casting long shadows. A little dusty and needing love, but full of
> character — the sense of an inherited space still being learned. Color palette
> dominated by deep plum-purples and warm lamp-gold (#2b2440 to #14101c range,
> with warm amber lamp highlights). Landscape aspect ratio (16:9). Cozy fantasy
> tone, not anime, not photorealistic. No people in the shot.
