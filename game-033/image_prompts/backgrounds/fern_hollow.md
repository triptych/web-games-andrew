# Background: fern_hollow

**Location:** The fern hollow north of Hollow's cottage, where the hedge wolf is
denning too close to the path — a green bowl of a place, close and dim and
quieter than the wood around it. Also where the spice root grows. Currently
reuses `whisperwood_edge.png` with its own gradient (`#35543c` → `#070c08`).

**Prompt:**

> A painterly digital illustration, storybook-style wide establishing shot (no
> characters or animals in frame) of a shallow bowl-shaped hollow in old woodland,
> filled waist-deep with bracken and ferns. Steep fern-covered banks rising on all
> sides, a fallen ash across one lip of the bowl, green light filtering down
> through a closed canopy so the whole scene reads underwater-green. Pressed flat
> patches in the ferns where something large has been lying. Utterly still. Colour
> palette mid-to-deep forest greens (#35543c to #131f16) with soft dappled
> highlights. Landscape aspect ratio (16:9). Cozy fantasy tone with a held breath
> in it, not horror. No people, no creatures.
