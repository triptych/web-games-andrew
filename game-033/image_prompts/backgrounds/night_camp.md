# Background: night_camp

**Location:** The same clearing after the hearth-stone is lit — the long night
the whole ritual actually consists of, sitting up with a fire the size of a
cupped hand until dawn. Used for the four companion scenes and the climax.
**No painted art exists yet** — gradient-only radial firelight (`#6b4a22` core →
`#06080c` edge).
