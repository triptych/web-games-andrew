# Portrait: thorn_crowned

**Character:** The Thorn-Crowned — not a monster and explicitly not evil: the
shape the Whisperwood takes when the agreement between wood and village has gone
four months untended and it has been grieving alone in a clearing. Can be fought,
quieted with Hearthbound Tea, or spoken to. Currently emoji stand-in: 🦌.

**Prompt:**

> A painterly digital illustration, storybook-style, of an enormous stag-shaped
> figure that is not a stag: a body assembled out of grey deadwood, old bramble
> and thorn by something working from a description of a stag. Its antlers go up
> and up into a dense crown of thorn. Where its eyes should be there are two small
> steady flames the size of a candle each — warm orange, gentle, wrong. It stands
> over a low stone with a fire in it, head lowered, and the whole posture reads as
> grief and exhaustion rather than menace: this is a sad enormous thing, not a
> predator. Deep night, the only light coming from the small fire below and the
> two flames. Near-black background (#06080c) with warm firelight rim (#6b4a22).
> Square-ish aspect ratio (1:1 or 4:5). Cozy fantasy tone with an edge of the
> uncanny — melancholy, not horror. Not anime, not photorealistic.
