# Portrait: ock_sly / ock_grin

**Character:** Peddler Ock — a travelling trader whose cart draws up by the well
from Day 2 onward. Small cart, optimistic awning, everything arranged to look
like there is more of it than there is. Enjoys being haggled with more than he
enjoys winning. Currently emoji stand-ins: 🧳 (sly) and 🤝 (grin).

**Prompt (sly):**

> A painterly digital illustration, storybook-style, of a wiry travelling
> merchant in his fifties, bust-up three-quarter view. Layered road-worn clothes
> in mismatched good fabrics, several straps and satchels, rings on two fingers,
> a hat he is slightly too proud of. His expression is shrewd and sidelong —
> mid-pitch, eyebrows up, already knowing what you are going to say. Bright
> midday market light. Background softly blurred/vignetted in slate-blue village
> square tones (#45566a wash). Square aspect ratio (1:1). Cozy fantasy tone, not
> anime, not photorealistic.

**Prompt (grin):**

> The same merchant, same clothes, caught genuinely delighted and slightly
> outmanoeuvred: a wide open grin, one hand extended to shake on a deal, head
> tipped back a little. He is enjoying having lost the argument. Same bright
> market light, same square aspect ratio, same painterly storybook treatment.
