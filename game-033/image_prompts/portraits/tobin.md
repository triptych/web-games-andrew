# Portrait: tobin_eager / tobin_scared

**Character:** Tobin — a village boy of about ten, obsessed with the wood and the
things in it, carries a stick he is clearly pretending is a sword. His dog
Biscuit has gone missing into the Whisperwood. He follows you into the deep wood
in the last act, which is the worst decision anyone makes all season. Currently
emoji stand-ins: 🧒 (eager) and 😨 (scared).

**Prompt (eager):**

> A painterly digital illustration, storybook-style, of a boy of about ten,
> bust-up three-quarter view. Scruffy fair hair, a too-big hand-me-down jerkin,
> dirt on one cheek, holding a peeled stick upright at his shoulder like a spear
> he has been told repeatedly not to carry. His expression is bright, over-eager,
> mid-sentence — telling you something enormous and mostly untrue. Warm daylight,
> village greens and browns. Background softly blurred/vignetted in slate-blue
> village tones (#45566a wash). Square aspect ratio (1:1). Cozy fantasy tone, not
> anime, not photorealistic.

**Prompt (scared):**

> The same boy, same clothes, but lit from below by small firelight in deep
> darkness — white to the lips, stick forgotten and hanging at his side, eyes
> enormous, the exact face of a child who has realised he is a very long way from
> home. Not a horror image: he is frightened, and the viewer is the person he has
> just found. Deep near-black surroundings with a small warm fire glow on his face
> (#06080c ground, #6b4a22 firelight). Same square aspect ratio, same painterly
> storybook treatment.
