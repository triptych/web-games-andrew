      How to Play

      Line it up

      Drag back from a teammate (like a slingshot or a pool cue) and let go. The dotted line shows where they will roll, the ring shows the first ball they will hit and where it will go.

      The world is frozen while you aim. Time only moves while something rolls, so every shot is also a move in a game of chess with the guards. WAIT lets time pass without moving.

      Stay out of sight

      White cones are what guards can see. Linger in one and a ? grows into a !: the alarm. Out of sight for long enough, they give up, search, then go back to their rounds. Hedges hide you if you stop in them. Hard hits against walls are loud.

      Takedowns

      Hit a guard from behind (or while he is busy) and he goes down. Banks off cushions first score style. Head-on, you need real speed. Knocked-out guards wake up eventually and their friends will notice the body: knock them into the water or a manhole and they are gone for good.
