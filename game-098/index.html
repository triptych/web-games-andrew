How to Play

Battles

Tap one of your units to see where it can go (blue). Tap a blue tile to move there, then choose an action: Attack (red), a Skill, Claim a village or roost, Unchain a collared dragon, or Wait. Tap the unit again to stay put. Every attack cuts to a close-up fight.

Damage depends on Attack against Defence, the element wheel (🔥 Ember › 🌿 Verdant › 🪨 Stone › ⚡ Storm › 💧 Tide › 🔥 Ember; ☀️ Lumen and 🌑 Umbra bane each other), body types (Skyhook ballistas shred fliers, lancers gore drakes), and the attacker's health: a wounded unit hits softer. Ground units take less damage on forests, hills, villages and mountains; fliers get no cover. The number on a unit is its health in tenths.

Claim villages and roosts: they pay 100 gold a turn and heal anyone of yours resting on them. On a roost you own, tap it to call another dragon from your stable. The Chain musters soldiers at its keeps the same way. Kestrel must survive: if the tamer falls, the battle is lost.

Unchain: wear a collared dragon below half health, stand Kestrel beside it and sing it free. Below a quarter it never fails. It joins you on the spot, and stays in your stable.

Wyrmsong: fighting fills the song meter. When it's full, tap 🎵 for a turn of power. Enemy commanders have powers too.

The Roost

Eggs hatch in incubators over real time, even while you fight or are away (up to 8 hours). Tap an egg to warm it faster. Feed dragons to level them, put them in a training ring to grow while you're idle, and collect the hoard your stable gathers. Adults (level 12) can breed: the egg inherits genes and traits from both parents, and some element pairs make rare hybrids.

Credits

Wyrmbond — a dragon-taming tactics RPG.

Every dragon, map, portrait, egg and note is made in code at runtime: three.js r165 for the 3D, Canvas 2D for the paint, and Web Audio for the music and sound. There are no image or audio files.

With a nod to Advance Wars, Fire Emblem, and every dragon-raising game that ever kept someone up past bedtime.
