Defend the Sol Gate. Wave after wave, bigger every time.

How to Play

Shoot everything. Dodge everything else. Hold fire for a stream of shots.

Arrows / WASDMove
Space / ZFire (hold)
X / B / ShiftBomb
P / EscPause

Touch: drag anywhere to move, hold FIRE (or leave auto-fire on), tap BOMB. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A fires, B bombs, Start pauses.

Power-ups

PPower: a bigger gun, up to five levels
SSpeed: a faster ship
BBomb: a rainbow shockwave that wipes the bullets
HShield: absorbs one hit
★Medal: worth more with each one you grab

Blinking enemies carry a power-up. Chain kills for a score multiplier. Every fourth wave is a bonus stage: nothing shoots, so hit them all. Every fifth wave is a boss. Lose a ship and your gun drops two levels.
