How to Play

Pyrrhax the Ember Wyrm sleeps in Mount Ember. When the laps run out he wakes, and your party must face him. Until then, roll round Emberwyld Isle and grow strong.

  • 🎲 Roll two dice and hop that many spaces. Doubles roll again. Passing Hearthstone pays you gold; landing there is a feast.
  • ⚔️ Encounters and roaming monsters start a turn-based battle. Pick Attack, Skills (cost MP), Items, Defend or Run. Hit weaknesses (🔥 ❄️ ⚡ ✨) for big damage. When the Valor gauge fills, unleash a Burst.
  • 🏘️ Towns: shop, rest at the inn, hire a companion at the tavern (up to two), and forge gear from monster materials. You may stop at a town or dungeon as you pass it.
  • 🏰 Dungeons: a battle, then a boss. Each boss guards a Dragonbane Shard; every shard weakens the dragon, and all four forge Dragonbane.
  • 🏡 Deeds: buy property. Rivals pay you a toll when they land there, you earn income every lap, and owning a whole region doubles tolls and gives a bonus.
  • 🃏 Fate cards, 🎁 chests (watch for mimics), the ⛩️ Shrine (rest and a blessing), the ⛲ Fountain (every lost coin) and the 🌀 Portal (go anywhere).
  • Lose a battle and you wake at the last corner, a little poorer. Lose to the dragon and you get one more lap to prepare.
