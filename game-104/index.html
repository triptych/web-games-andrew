Hemlock Hollow

A gothic mystery of herbs, hedge-magic
and a town that sells its cures

How to Play

You are Elspeth, a hedge witch's apprentice. Your mentor, Marga, is gone. Find her.

Type short commands, as in the old adventures: look, take satchel, go north, talk to bryony, ask moll about marga, use salve on bryony, read note, cast kindle. On a phone, tap the buttons: pick a verb, then a thing.

Herbs. Smell, study and pick plants. Brew with the kettle: brew yarrow and plantain. Experiment, but never taste what is black or purple-spotted.

Magic. Your ember powers spells; rest to refill it. Dread rises when you are noticed. At the top, the Crows take you. When you hear boots, hide.

Type hint if you are stuck, save or restore any time.
