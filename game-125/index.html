SEEDFALL

Forty thousand sleepers. One year. One planet that bites.

How to Play

You wake alone on the colony ship Ark Meridian. In 365 days every stasis pod opens. Make the planet below, Cradle, fit to live on by then: an Eden Index of 80, and the Heartroot settled.

  1. Drop a bot from the Hangar. You see through its eyes: gather metal, crystal and plants, fight what fights you, and walk back to the beacon.
  2. Sequence genomes from the plants and creatures you sample, then splice two in the Gene Lab. Release the strain on a zone: it scrubs toxins, calms the fauna and greens the soil.
  3. Research blueprints, better strains, harvesters and colonies. It all runs on game days, even while a bot is down.
  4. Build the next generation of bot to reach the next zone. Six generations, six zones.
