How to Play

Move like chess pieces. Every piece in your party has its own movement: the Rook slides, the Knight leaps, the Grasshopper vaults over a piece, the Cannon fires over a screen. Tap a piece to see where it can go.

Capture to duel. Move onto an enemy and the board gives way to a Fire Emblem-style duel: strike, counter, and a follow-up for whoever is 4+ speed faster. Win, and you take its square. Lose the exchange and a survivor is pushed back.

Elements. 🔥 Ember beats 🌿 Thorn beats 🌊 Tide beats 🔥 Ember. 🌘 Umbra and ✦ Lumen hurt each other. Advantage: +25% damage and +15 hit.

Abilities. Each piece has one special ability instead of an action; it then rests a few rounds.
