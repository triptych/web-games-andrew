How to Play

Read the story: click, tap, Space or Enter. Choices change how your friends feel about you.

Free time: walk the campus and press A (Space / Enter / tap) at a place. Hanging out, playing a friend's mini-game, training or exploring takes up the afternoon or the evening; gifts, shopping and quests don't.

Hearts: spend time with someone and give them gifts they love to fill their hearts and unlock their heart events.

Battles: on a hero's turn choose Strike, a Skill, an Item or Defend. Kai's Echo copies the last spell anyone cast, even an enemy's, at 125%.

Echo Charms: twelve are hidden around the island. Find them all.

Arrows / WASDWalk · mini-game directions
