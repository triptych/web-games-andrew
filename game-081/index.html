How to Play

In the world

WASD / arrowsWalk (Shift to hurry); ← → also turn
MouseLook (click the view to lock the pointer)
E / Space / clickTalk, use, open
J / TabJournal: skills, codex, bestiary, evidence
Esc / PPause

Touch: drag the left side to walk (double-tap and hold to hurry), drag the right side to look, tap ✋ to use. Gamepad: sticks, A to use, Y for the journal, Start to pause.

In the Lumen Lens

Every glowing mote inside the patient is something that hurts. Drag from mote to neighbouring mote (diagonals count). Each hop must share something you can sense: the same form (shape), and later the same aura (colour), depth (size) or nature (the ring behind it). Let go with three or more to treat them. Each trail costs one Focus; finish the goals at the top before you run out.

Trails of seven grow a Lifebloom that links to anything. Treated motes pour into the vials; a full vial is a power: tap it, then tap the grid. Remedies from the apothecary shelf help in a pinch. Keyboard: arrows move, Space picks, Enter treats, H for a hint.
