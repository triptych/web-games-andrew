Hearthwick

A cozy little town-builder. One hut, one neighbour, a lot of trees.

How to Play

Tap trees, boulders and bushes to gather by hand. Drag to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom.

Open Build (🔨) to put up a Woodshed, then give villagers a job in it. Buildings keep working while you do other things, and even while you are away.

Follow the Journal (📜): every entry you finish pays out and, with enough Renown, lets you upgrade your Hall to unlock more of the town.

Build the Adventurers' Lodge and hire heroes. They go on quests, come home hungry and tired, eat at your Tavern, sleep at your Inn and buy gear from your Item Shop. Craft the gear, cook the meals, stock the shelves. Raiders come at dusk now and then: heroes at home defend the town.
