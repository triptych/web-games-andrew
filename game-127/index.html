How to Play

You are the Warden of Cinderford. The old King has placed his grandson in your care: a ten-year-old half-dragon whom the court will judge on his eighteenth hatch-day. What he becomes is up to you.

Each month

  1. Free Day. Three actions in the city: visit a friend (bring a gift), talk with the prince, shop, take a quest from the Guild Hall, pray, see a play…
  2. Plan. Fill three periods with lessons (cost gold), jobs (earn gold), rest, quests or expeditions.
  3. Watch it unfold. Stats rise, gold changes hands, and Stress builds. Too much Stress and he skips lessons, falls ill, and the dragon in him grows louder.

The heart

Six childhood friends grow up beside him. Visit them, learn their tastes, and their four heart scenes open with time. Win one's heart completely and they may stand beside him at the end, and if he becomes King, on the throne.
