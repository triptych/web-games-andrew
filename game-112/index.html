Adventure Module Q1 · for the General of one Legion

Quill & Legion

Recruit the host. Draw the order. Ink the battle.

How to Play

You are a General with a legion of creature stacks: twenty-two pikemen are one stack, five dire wolves another. A stack's number is its strength: it hits harder the more of them there are, and loses them as it is hurt. Losses stay lost between battles. Win gold and recruit more at dwellings.

The march

Three books, each a map of roads ending in a boss. Choose your path: skirmishes and lairs (harder, with a relic), dwellings to recruit, the scriptorium to buy and erase orders, campfires to rest or inscribe a card, hoards and strange encounters.

The battle

Deploy your stacks in your two columns, then fight in turns. Each turn you draw 5 orders and have 3 ink. Every stack may move once for free (tap it, then a gold hex) and attack once, but attacks, spells and tactics are cards: pick a card, then who carries it out, then the target.

Foes show their intent above them: what they will do and for how much. Walkers march up to strike; archers shoot from anywhere (at half with a foe beside them). A stack struck in melee strikes back once a round. Every other stack of yours touching a foe adds 20% to blows against it. Ward soaks damage until your next turn.

Each creature brings its own signature card to your deck. If the stack is wiped out, the card goes with it.
