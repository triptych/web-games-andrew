Read the ball. Lay the cards. Learn what the universe is whispering, and save a king.

How to Play

You have inherited Madame Orla’s fortune-teller’s wagon. People of Hollowmere come to your table with worries. For each one:

  1. Listen. What they say is a clue: their worry is the matter now.
  2. Gaze. The crystal ball shows three visions: what was, what is, what comes. A symbol names the matter; its colour gives the tone: some colours bring a boon, some a bane. A clouded vision hides its symbol. Read the omen in the room, trust the client’s worry, or spend a Focus.
  3. Lay three cards, one for each vision. A card answers a matter if it carries it. Upright brings a boon; reversed brings a bane. Right matter but wrong way up is half right.
  4. Speak. Plain truth, gentle words or a grand show: clients have a favourite way to hear it.

Everything you learn goes into the Codex (C). Spend an evening studying, or with someone in the village. The King is ill, and the Second Prince will come looking for a seer.
