How to Play

I'm Pip, your cannoneer! Knock over every little person in the castle to clear it. Tip them over, bonk them, or drop the floor out from under them. Smash more blocks for more stars!

The cannonballs

  • Iron Ball: good, honest smashing.
  • Boom Ball: explodes when it hits. Tap in flight to go off early.
  • Triple Trouble: tap in flight to split into three.
  • Big Bertha: slow, huge, unstoppable.
  • Boing Ball: bounces all over the place.
