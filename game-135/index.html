Hard Hat Harbor

A toy box of trucks. A town to rebuild.

How to Play

Pick a job from the job board, climb into the right vehicle from the toy box, and follow the yellow arrow.

↑ ↓ / W SDrive forward, reverse
← → / A DSteer
SpaceWork: tip, dig, pour, lift, swing (hold to dig and pour). Otherwise: honk!
1 – 8, Q / ESwap vehicle
JJob board
P / EscPause

Touch: drag on the left to drive, press the big button to work, tap a vehicle to swap. Gamepad: stick or triggers, A to work, bumpers to swap, Y for jobs, Start to pause.

Finish a job quickly for three stars. Nothing can go wrong: take your time.
