Sugarplum
Drop

A Christmas candy merge puzzle

How to Play

Drop treats into the jar. When two of the same treat touch, they merge into the next one up, and the new one can merge again. Fill the jar past the striped line for too long and the game is over.

Two golden Jingle Bells make a Christmas Miracle: both vanish for a big bonus. Merges in quick succession make a chain and score extra.
