KAIJU ROLL

Curl up. Roll out. Flatten the city.

How to Play

You are a kaiju curled into a ball. You never stop rolling: steer to curve your path and bounce off buildings, like the ball in a brick-breaker. Flatten 80% of a city to move on.

Arrows / WASDSteer the ball
Space / ZPounce: hop, then slam. Smashes everything near where you land, swats helicopters and dodges shells
P / EscPause
  • Eat small things: people, cars, trees and rubble stick to you and make you bigger. A bigger ball hits much harder and rolls right over houses.
  • Floors fall off buildings one at a time. Fast hits do the most damage.
  • Tanks, helicopters and bombers fight back. Jump over shells and missiles. Roll through a tank to flatten it.
  • Power-ups drop from fallen buildings: 🔥 fire, 🧲 magnet, 🛡️ shield, 🍔 meal, 💖 hug.
  • Snack carts and pizza trucks heal you. Chain smashes for a score multiplier.

Touch: drag on the left half to steer, tap POUNCE. Gamepad: stick or d-pad, A to pounce, Start to pause.
