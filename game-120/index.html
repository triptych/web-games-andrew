Touch: left thumb walks, right thumb aims and fires; FIRE auto-aims. Gamepad: sticks, RT fire, B missiles, A jump, LB boost.

Shoot orange pods to free the colonists. Red circles are incoming blasts, and expanding rings are shockwaves: jump over them. Collect scrap, spend it in the hangar between missions, and the walker grows with every upgrade.
