How to Play

  • Tap anything in the room to look at it or pick it up.
  • To use an item, tap it in your pack, then tap something in the room. Tap two pack items to combine them.
  • Tap doors to move between rooms. 🗺️ jumps straight to any room you have opened.
  • 📓 holds your clues, the case board and the codes. 💡 gives a hint, a little clearer each time you ask. ✨ makes everything you can tap sparkle.
  • Nothing here can hurt you or get lost. Wrong tries just get a silly line.
  • Look out for a shy little ghost hiding in every room.
