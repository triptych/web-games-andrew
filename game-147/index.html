Inn Over My Head

A cozy inn, a fussy dragon banker, and 40,000 gold to go.

How to Play

You've inherited the Drowsy Dragonfly Inn, and 40,000 gold of debt to Madame Goldmaw, payable in five installments. Fill the rooms, cook, clean and adventure to pay it off.

🛎️ The inn

  • Guests wait at the Desk. Check them into a room; they pay when they leave and tip while they stay. A spotless room and the meal they asked for earn bigger tips and reputation.
  • Dust bunnies breed in the rooms. Click (or tap) them to shoo them. Clean rooms raise the ✨ Sparkle rating, and a spotless inn gives the party Fresh Linens (+10% Speed and Evasion) on its next adventure.
  • Kitchen: pick a recipe, then press Stir! when the needle is in the gold zone. Meals sell to guests, or the party eats one before an adventure for a buff. A cook on staff makes everything better.
  • Strangers arrive with a request. Give them a room, cook their favourite and do their task, and they'll join as party, staff or both. Staff keep working while you're away.
  • The clock never stops: tips, dust, herbs and staff keep going while you fight, and even while the game is closed.

⚔️ Battles

  • The bar at the top is the turn order. On your turn: Attack, a Skill, a Chain Attack, an Item, Defend or Flee.
  • Hit a monster's weakness (shown under its name) to break its guard. At zero it's Staggered: it loses a turn, takes +30% damage, and opens a free Chain Attack.
  • Hits fill the Chain gauge; when it's full, any hero can launch a Chain Attack. Close bonds unlock Duo and Trio chains.
  • Hit an enemy with one element and then another hero's element to set off a combo (fire then breeze makes a Firestorm…).
  • Auto battles for you: Balanced, Aggressive or Conserve MP.
