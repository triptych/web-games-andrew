    How to Play

    It's chess. Every rule is the real one: castling, en passant, promotion, check and mate. The Royal Navy (white) sails first. Tap or click one of your ships to see where it can go, then tap a square. Drag to swing the camera; pinch or scroll to zoom.

    The fleets

    The sea plays too

    After a move there's a chance (set by the sea state) that something happens. The sea is wild but fair: it never takes a Flagship, never moves a Lighthouse (unless the Kraken drags one under), never leaves the side that just moved in check, and never ends the game by itself.

    Keys

    Arrows + Enter: move a cursor and select · U undo · H hint · F turn board · L labels · M sound · N music · Esc menu
