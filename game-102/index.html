How to Play

Every robot on the station has to go. Pull the sling back, let go, and let physics do the rest.

Drag + releasePull back from anywhere, let go to fire
↑ ↓Raise or lower the aim
← →Less or more power
Space / ZFire; in flight, the asteroid's special
Tap in flightThe special: Rock burns, Iron dives, Ice splits, Nova detonates
P / EscPause

Glass breaks easily, hull takes a pounding, fuel cells blow up. Black holes bend your shots. Asteroids left over when the last robot falls are worth bonus points.
