Lanternhollow

a cozy little dungeon builder

Roll a seed and watch tunnels, chambers, waterfalls and wells settle into place, one level under another. Fill them with sleepy monsters and glittering hoards, give every level its own stone or wood or crystal, then share it with a link and walk it by lantern light.

How it works

Every level is grown by wave function collapse: little 3×3 tiles (tunnels, chamber walls, pools, waterfalls, wells) are placed only where their edges match their neighbours, until the whole map is decided. Then the generator checks that every corner can be reached from the stairs, and tries again if not.

🗺️ ShapeSeed, size, levels and the mix of tunnels, chambers, water and wells.
🐾 ElementsHow many monsters, hoards, lights, cozy things, flora and peek windows.
🎨 ThemesWood, stone, brick, rock, moss, crystal or frost, level by level.
🖌️ PaintTap a square to make it a chamber, tunnel, water, well or rock. The rest regrows around it.
🧸 PlaceTap the floor to put down any element. Tidy takes one away.
DepthThe slider on the right moves between levels (or keys 1–5, PgUp/PgDn).
Walk itDrag or click to look, WASD or the stick to walk. Visit every friend and find every hoard; stairs take you down.

Look closely at the barred windows and down the wells: those rooms are drawn by interior mapping, and the stones and planks by parallax occlusion mapping. The monsters and treasures were made with Meshy.
