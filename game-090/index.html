How to Play

Explore

Walk with WASD, arrows or the touch stick. Space / A uses what is in front of you (stairs, chests, Sprouts) or swings. Strike a Hex first and the whole group starts the fight knocked down. Let one catch you from behind and it ambushes you.

Press Turn

Each side gets one icon per member standing. Hit a weakness or land a critical and the foe is knocked down and the hit costs only half an icon, so you act again. Missing, being nulled, or being repelled costs you more. When every foe is down: All-Out Attack.

Recode

Choose Recode on a Hex and talk it round. Each personality answers one script best: Soothe, Command, Barter, Mirror. Reach full rapport before it loses patience and it joins you. Weakened and knocked-down Hexes listen more easily, and the tide of the moon helps or hurts.

Splice

In the Greenhouse, two daemons become one. The race of the result comes from the splice table, its level from the average, and it can inherit skills. Preview first.
