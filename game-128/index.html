Gemheart Saga

A match-3 fantasy RPG

Choose your hero

Each hero walks the Vale in a different order, meets different people and hunts a different enemy.

Briarwick

How to Play

Battles. Swap two neighbouring gems to line up three or more. The board never stops: gems fall and cascades chain while you keep swapping. Every match hits the foe or helps you:

    Match 4 in a line for a line gem, an L or T for a bomb, 5 in a line for a prism, which clears a whole colour when you swap it. The foe attacks when its bar runs out. Some turn gems to stone, chain them, or plant doom gems that burst if you don't match next to them in time. Under its timer the foe shows what its next attack brings, and a warning flashes before it lands.

    Skills spend mana: tap them under the board, or press 1 2 3. Items are 4 5 6.

    Briarwick. Between quests, build and upgrade the village. The Inn, Farm, Lumber Mill, Mine and Mage Tower produce while you're away. Claim the daily gift every day for a growing streak and a two-hour harvest festival. The villagers' news along the bottom says what to do next: tap a hint to go there. Tap a resource at the top to see how to get more.
