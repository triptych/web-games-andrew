Kills fill Fury. Fire Overdrive for eight seconds of slow motion: you hit harder and every kill heals you. Chain kills for combos. Guns drop from everything: every one is rolled from a maker, a rarity, parts and an element. Find the keycards, find the exit, and find the three VHS tapes on every level: they tell you who really killed Colonel Kane.

Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb looks; FIRE, JUMP (hold to fly), KICK and the small buttons. Gamepad: sticks, RT fire, A jump, B kick, X reload/swap, Y next gun, LB bomb, RB Overdrive, Start pause.
