How to Play

  • Dig: tap or click a block that touches open floor. Harder blocks take several strikes. Every strike costs 1 stamina.
  • Read the numbers: a number on open floor counts the traps and monsters hidden in the eight blocks around it.
  • Mark a block you think is dangerous (right-click, long-press, or the ⚑ Mark button then tap). Striking a marked trap disarms it; a marked monster is ambushed. A false alarm only costs a little stamina.
  • Monsters hit back after every strike you make until you beat them. Tap them to fight.
  • Puzzles: strike rune blocks in numeral order; turn sun channels until light reaches the sun panel; light all five runes of a chest lock; find the key for a bronze door.
  • Clear the floor (every block, monster and chest) and the hatch opens to the next one.
  • Out of stamina or knocked out? You are hauled up to camp to rest. Nothing is ever lost below. No hit takes you from healthy to fainted in one go.
  • At camp: buy from Zahra, spend skill points, claim quests, and admire your museum.
