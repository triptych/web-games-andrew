STARSPIN

A space fantasy slot machine roguelike

How to Play

The House stole the stars. Your ship's Fortune Engine is a slot machine, and every symbol on its payline fires.

  1. SPIN lands every reel.
  2. Spend Energy to nudge a reel one symbol up or down (▲ ▼), or respin it (⟳). You can see the symbols above and below the line.
  3. FIRE. Matching symbols multiply each other: a pair ×1.5 each, three ×2, four ×2.5, five ×3. Wild Stars join the biggest group.
  4. Enemies then do what their tag showed. Tap an enemy to make it your target.

After each win, add a symbol to every reel (the reels are your deck), then light a star on the Constellation. It is built like a Slay the Spire map: each star must join the last one you lit, so your path decides your build. Waystations let you repair or strip a symbol out.
